About the Garden

A Living Oasis in
Miami Beach

Nestled in the heart of Miami Beach, the Garden is a free tropical sanctuary. As a nonprofit, we welcome everyone who seeks beauty, tranquility, and a genuine connection with nature.

From quiet paths and water features to tropical palms and seasonal blooms — the Garden is a living classroom, community gathering space, and a refuge for biodiversity in the city.
