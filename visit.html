Plan Your Visit

Come &

Miami Beach Botanical Garden is a three-acre tropical oasis in the heart of Miami Beach. Admission is free, and visitors are welcome Tuesday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

2000 Convention Ctr Dr, Miami Beach, FL 33139
  • Open Tuesday–Sunday
  • 9:00 AM–5:00 PM
  • Free admission
  • ADA-compliant pathways
  • Pets allowed on leash
  • Guided tours by advance request

Getting Here

Parking &
Directions

Parking is available along nearby streets, in adjacent lots, and in Miami Beach Convention Center garages.

  • Street parking — metered spots along 19th Street, with more parking near Meridian Avenue and Convention Center Drive.
  • Garages — Convention Center garages on 19th and 20th Streets may offer lower hourly rates.
  • Rooftop parking — may be available at the Convention Center. Enter from the north end of Convention Center Drive near Dade Boulevard, take the elevator to the first floor, and follow signs to the Garden.
  • Validation — Garden visitors may receive discounted parking; redeem your validation at the Botanical Boutique.

Accessibility

The Garden Is
for Everyone

The Garden's pathways are ADA compliant, though some areas include slight elevation changes, natural stone pavers, and varied walking surfaces. Wheelchairs are available at the Visitor Center with a photo ID and credit card.

Garden Guidelines

A few simple ways to help us keep the Garden beautiful.

No Smoking

The Garden is a smoke-free space.

Leashed Pets Welcome

Pets and service animals must remain leashed.

Walk, Don't Ride

No bikes, skates, skateboards, or micro-mobility devices inside the Garden.

Kids with Adults

Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.

Stay on the Paths

Please stay out of garden beds and private areas.

Look, Don't Pick

Do not pick flowers, fruit, or plant material.
