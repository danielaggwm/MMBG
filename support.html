Cultivator

$75 / year

Best for individuals who want to support the Garden and enjoy basic member benefits.

  • Reciprocal admission to participating gardens
  • Discounts on select classes and workshops
  • 10% off Botanical Boutique purchases
  • Free admission to Jazz in the Jungle
  • Garden newsletters

Pollinator

$250 / year

Best for members who want to attend more events and bring a guest.

  • All Cultivator benefits

  • Garden-branded gift
