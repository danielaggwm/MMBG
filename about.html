About the Garden

A Living Oasis
in Miami Beach

Miami Beach Botanical Garden is an urban oasis and micro-forest in the heart of Miami Beach. Through education, wellness, cultural programming, and immersive experiences, we inspire people of all ages to connect with nature and care for the environment.

Our Story

A Garden Rooted in
Miami Beach History

The Garden began in 1962 as a city garden center across from the Miami Beach Convention Center. Its site is connected to the historic Collins Canal, Miami Beach's early agricultural past, and the city's transformation into a cultural and tourist destination.

After years of change and storm damage, the Garden was reimagined with the help of landscape architect Raymond Jungles, whose redesign emphasized native plants, water conservation, ecological design, and a more immersive public garden experience.

Today, the Garden is owned by the City of Miami Beach and operated by the Conservancy, serving as a sanctuary for nature, learning, culture, and community.
